# Contributing

Keep contributions small, reusable, and free of study-specific or controlled material.

1. Do not add research data, private results, manuscripts, full-text literature, credentials, or personal absolute paths.
2. Treat method notes as qualified examples. Record status, scope, assumptions, software dependencies, evidence sources, and which rules are only local preferences.
3. Do not add empty folders or templates in anticipation of possible future use.
4. Run `python3 tools/workflow_toolkit.py check` before proposing a change.
5. Do not commit generated manuscript outputs or temporary render files.

Use the repository's `AGENTS.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`, and `QUALITY_CONTROL.md` as the governing maintenance and quality rules.
